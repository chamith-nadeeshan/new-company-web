FAQs

Questions, answered

Can't find what you need? Send us a message.

How long does a typical project take?+

Websites usually take 3–6 weeks. Web and mobile apps range from 8–16 weeks depending on scope.

Do you work with clients outside your country?+

Yes. We work remotely with clients across time zones and schedule overlap hours for calls.

Who owns the code and design files?+

You do. Full ownership of source code, designs, and accounts transfers on final payment.

Can you take over an existing project?+

Yes. We start with a code and infrastructure audit, then propose a stabilisation plan.

What happens after launch?+

Every plan includes a support window, and we offer monthly retainers for ongoing work.

How do payments work?+

Typically 40% upfront, 40% at design or beta sign-off, and 20% on launch.
